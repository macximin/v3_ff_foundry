This profile covers martial arts and murim works. It routes feedback where
status, force, reputation, and public proof carry the reader reward.

## Commercial Assets

- Power must be proved in public or through consequences people recognize.
- Rank matters: sect hierarchy, master-disciple lines, clan standing, alliances,
  debt, face, and public humiliation are currencies.
- Receipts are concrete: stance, wound, weapon, manual, qi/internal energy
  trace, broken formation, duel result, witness reaction.
- Fight rhythm and spectator pressure should carry payoff, not abstract
  cultivation explanation alone.
- Honor, debt, and face must behave like tradeable pressure, not moral lectures.

## Stage Routing

### pitch

Ask whether the protagonist's martial edge can reverse hierarchy quickly enough
to hook the reader.

### story

Check that the first anchors have a visible arena: sect, duel, debt, grudge,
ranking, or public recognition pressure.

### episode_bet

Every episode should know what proof is gained: martial proof, social rank,
fear, debt repayment, public humiliation, weapon/manual claim, or new enemy
misread.

### manuscript

Look for impact, stance, breath, injury, witness reaction, face loss, and the
social consequence of force.

### review

Harvest murim failures as genre scope. Do not rewrite them as corporate paperwork
or modern negotiation logic.

## Do Not

- Do not make sect politics read like office bureaucracy unless the work is
  deliberately hybrid.
- Do not replace public proof with private self-labeling.
- Do not let cultivation terminology become setup-only exposition.
- Do not promote one sect's etiquette into universal craft.
